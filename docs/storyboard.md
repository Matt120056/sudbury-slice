Sudbury Slice: Storyboard
Persona: Steph field, a 34-year-old college student living in Sudbury who has a long day of
classes and wants to find an affordable pizza nearby. she prefers vegetarian toppings and wants to avoid wasting money on a pizza she might not enjoy.

Panel 1: Finding Something to Eat
Scene: Steph finishes her afternoon classes and realizes she is hungry but does not have time to cook. She opens Sudbury Slice to find a good pizza option nearby.
Screen: Home screen showing available pizzas and popular choices that have ratings attached to them.
Data used: Reads pizzas to display available pizzas and the ratings to show the highest-rated choices.

Panel 2: Choosing a Pizza
Scene: Steph browses the pizza menu and finds a vegetarian pizza that looks tasty. She checks the ingredients to make sure it does not contain meat or cheese.
Screen: Pizza details screen showing the pizza description, price, ingredients, and available toppings.
Data used: Reads pizzas to get pizza details and toppings to display the ingredients and available customization options.

Panel 3: Customizing Her Order
Scene: Steph goes to customize her pizza by adding extra mushrooms and green peppers. She removes any toppings she does not want before confirming her choice.
Screen: Pizza customization screen with selectable toppings.
Data used: Reads pizzas and toppings to display customization options and writes the selected customer and pizza information to
 the appropriate order or customer records, depending on the app's data design.

Panel 4: Rating Her Pizza
Scene: After enjoying her meal, Steph returns to Sudbury Slice and gives the vegetarian pizza a five-star rating. She wants other students to know that it is a good option.
Screen: Pizza review and rating screen.
Data used: Writes to ratings with the customer's ID, pizza ID, star rating, and review. Reads customers and pizzas to associate the rating with the correct person and pizza.

Panel 5: Checking Popular Pizzas
Scene: A few days later, Steph opens the app again to see which pizzas are popular with other customers. She checks the ratings before deciding what to try next.
Screen: Popular pizzas screen showing average ratings and customer reviews.
Data used: Reads ratings to calculate average scores and display reviews. Reads pizzas to display pizza names and details and
customers to identify customers who submitted reviews.
